Statistical methods

The estimand, the encoder, the evidence model, the calibration, and the conditions each rests on.

Scope

CHORALE first expresses every modality on a fixed vocabulary of named biological concepts. It then estimates concept-level phenotype effects, concept-family effects, a low-rank joint representation of the stacked concept scores, held-out transfer of those directions, and phenotype-linked free dimensions outside the vocabulary. These are separate estimands sharing one encoding, not interchangeable versions of one result.

A concept is a set of features defined before results are inspected. The modalities are measured on different individuals, so the vocabulary supplies a common reference frame; no individual-level cross-modality profile is observed or reconstructed.

Notation is introduced where it is used. Modalities are indexed by mmm, concepts by ccc, samples within modality mmm by iii, and features of modality mmm by jjj.

The vocabulary and its coverage

A vocabulary is a collection of sets Cc\mathcal{C}_cCc​ of feature identifiers. For modality mmm these become a feature-by-concept weight matrix WmW_mWm​, whose entry wjcw_{jc}wjc​ is the weight with which feature jjj carries concept ccc. A gene or protein identifier matching nnn identifiers in the reference space contributes 1/n1/n1/n to each, so a one-to-many map contributes proportionally rather than counting once per match. A lipid species carries a concept when its class takes part in the pathway that names the concept.

Modality mmm expresses concept ccc when #{j:wjc>0}\#\{j : w_{jc} > 0\}#{j:wjc​>0} reaches a declared minimum. The counts are reported per concept and per modality, since a concept a modality cannot express carries no evidence there.

The design does not enter this step. Scoring therefore cannot be shaped by the contrast the scores are subsequently tested on.

Curated concepts overlap, so the columns of SmS_mSm​ are correlated and the coefficients of a regression on them are close to arbitrary. What the overlap does not make arbitrary is the space the scores span, and it is that space which decides what is left over. How many free dimensions

The count is selected by reproducibility. At each candidate count kkk the decomposition is run from several initialisations and on several subsamples of the samples. Components of every run are matched one to one against a reference run by absolute correlation of loadings, solved as a linear assignment problem, and the matched correlation is recorded per component. Loadings rather than scores are compared, because a run on a subsample has no scores for the samples it left out.

The selected count is the largest kkk such that every count up to and including it has its weakest component above a declared threshold. Two bounds cap the search. Parallel analysis on RmR_mRm​, comparing observed eigenvalues with those of column-permuted data, gives the first. A cap of one component per five samples gives the second, since beyond it the recovered axes describe individual samples.

Where the realised concept-score matrix has full row rank on the centred sample space, its projection is the identity on that space and Rm=0R_m = 0Rm​=0 up to numerical precision. Having at least as many concepts as samples makes full row rank possible, but does not guarantee it: correlated or redundant concept scores can have lower rank. CHORALE therefore decides from the residual norm, not from the dimensions alone. No free dimensions are fitted when that residual is numerical dust.

Adjusted design effects

For concept ccc in modality mmm, let ymcy_{mc}ymc​ be its score vector. CHORALE fits

ymc=Dmβmc+εmc,y_{mc} = D_m \beta_{mc} + \varepsilon_{mc},ymc​=Dm​βmc​+εmc​,

where DmD_mDm​ carries an intercept, the phenotype contrasts, and every covariate the modalities share and can jointly estimate. Continuous covariates enter as standardised slopes; a categorical covariate enters as treatment contrasts on the levels every modality realises, so a level observed in one cohort and not another cannot enter the comparison. The reported phenotype coefficient is adjusted for the rest of DmD_mDm​.

A covariate absent from a modality, incompatible in type or levels, more than half missing, or not jointly estimable after the covariates already retained is excluded with its reason recorded.

No correspondence between modalities is estimated at any point. That is what the fixed vocabulary removes from the problem.

Calibration and error control

The quantity under test is the phenotype coefficient of a model that also carries the adjusting covariates, so the null must be a null for that coefficient. Shuffling raw phenotype labels would break its relation to those covariates as well, giving designs the study could not have produced.

The null is therefore the one of Freedman and Lane (1983). Write Dm=[Zm,Pm]D_m = [Z_m, P_m]Dm​=[Zm​,Pm​], with ZmZ_mZm​ the intercept and the adjusting covariates and PmP_mPm​ the phenotype contrasts. The reduced model ymc=Zmγmc+rmcy_{mc} = Z_m\gamma_{mc} + r_{mc}ymc​=Zm​γmc​+rmc​ is fitted, its residuals are permuted, and the response is rebuilt as

ymc∗=Zmγ^mc+Πrmc,y^{*}_{mc} = Z_m\hat{\gamma}_{mc} + \Pi r_{mc},ymc∗​=Zm​γ^​mc​+Πrmc​,

with Π\PiΠ a permutation matrix. The full model is refitted to ymc∗y^{*}_{mc}ymc∗​ and its phenotype coefficient recorded. The design matrix never changes, so the phenotype keeps exactly the relation to the covariates it has in the data, and only the part of the score the covariates do not explain is exchanged. The same Π\PiΠ is applied to every concept of a modality, so the correlation between concepts that share features is carried into the null rather than assumed away.

Π\PiΠ is unrestricted unless the study declares which samples are exchangeable. Blocks are read only from the columns named in chorale_control(exchangeability_blocks = ), because a repeated measure, a litter or a processing batch is a fact about the study. Blocks are not inferred from the design. A continuous covariate would give one sample per block, and the permutation would then be either the identity, or a permutation over covariates dropped until it is not. Neither is a statement about the study.

The encoder is never refitted: SmS_mSm​ is computed once and only its response is rebuilt. The number of permutations BBB is therefore set by the resolution required rather than by what a refit can afford, and the smallest attainable p-value is 1/(B+1)1/(B+1)1/(B+1).

Two quantities come from the same permutations. Writing Zc(b)Z^{(b)}_cZc(b)​ for the statistic of concept ccc under permutation bbb,

pc=1+#{b:∣Zc(b)∣≥∣Zc∣}B+1,pcfam=1+#{b:max⁡c′∣Zc′(b)∣≥∣Zc∣}B+1.p_c = \frac{1 + \#\{b : |Z^{(b)}_c| \ge |Z_c|\}}{B+1}, \qquad p^{\text{fam}}_c = \frac{1 + \#\{b : \max_{c'} |Z^{(b)}_{c'}| \ge |Z_c|\}}{B+1}.pc​=B+11+#{b:∣Zc(b)​∣≥∣Zc​∣}​,pcfam​=B+11+#{b:maxc′​∣Zc′(b)​∣≥∣Zc​∣}​.

The second controls the family-wise error rate over the vocabulary, exactly under the dependence that overlapping concepts have, because the permutations carry the same overlap the data do. The false discovery rate at threshold ttt is estimated from the same array as

FDR^(t)=B−1#{(b,c′):∣Zc′(b)∣≥t}#{c′:∣Zc′∣≥t}.\widehat{\mathrm{FDR}}(t) = \frac{B^{-1}\#\{(b,c') : |Z^{(b)}_{c'}| \ge t\}}{\#\{c' : |Z_{c'}| \ge t\}} .FDR(t)=#{c′:∣Zc′​∣≥t}B−1#{(b,c′):∣Zc′(b)​∣≥t}​.

Each concept’s q-value is the smallest such estimate over thresholds that would also call it, which makes the reported rates monotone in the statistic.

Free dimensions are calibrated the same way, with the maximum taken over free dimensions rather than over concepts, so the two families are controlled separately and neither borrows the other’s null.

The modality shuffle is reported as a description rather than as a test. Three differences separate a shuffled collection from the fit. Pooling keeps only the features the modalities share, so the shuffled collection is scored on a smaller feature space and often on a smaller vocabulary. The reallocation preserves neither each modality’s sample count nor its design composition. The observed statistic was computed on the full assays. No error rate follows from that comparison.

Concept families

Let GgG_gGg​ be a family fixed from vocabulary overlap before testing. Complete linkage at Jaccard threshold aaa ensures that every pair in a multi-concept family overlaps by at least aaa. Ungrouped concepts remain singletons.

For one phenotype term, the family statistic is

Tg=∣∑c∈GgZc∣∣Gg∣.T_g = \frac{\left|\sum_{c \in G_g} Z_c\right|}{\sqrt{|G_g|}}.Tg​=∣Gg​∣​​∑c∈Gg​​Zc​​​.

The signed sum makes concordant concepts accumulate and discordant concepts cancel. For permutation bbb, the same function is applied to the signed statistics Zc(b)Z_c^{(b)}Zc(b)​ already generated for concept inference. This retains dependence induced by shared features. p_value compares a family with its own null; p_family compares it with the largest family statistic in each permutation; q_value estimates FDR over families. A supported family is a group-level result and does not imply that each member is supported.

Joint low-rank state

Concept meta-analysis combines scalar effects estimated separately. Joint-state analysis instead factorises all concept-score observations at once. Let ZmZ_mZm​ contain the scores of modality mmm, after optional regression on modality-local nuisance variables and centring and scaling each concept within that modality. The blocks are stacked by row to form ZZZ. Let Ω\OmegaΩ mark the entries that were observed; a modality-concept combination below the coverage threshold is missing, not zero.

For rank kkk, chorale_joint_state() alternates ridge-regularised least-squares updates for sample scores UUU and concept loadings VVV, using only Ωic=1\Omega_{ic}=1Ωic​=1:

∑(i,c):Ωic=1(Zic−uiTvc)2.\sum_{(i,c):\Omega_{ic}=1} (Z_{ic} - u_i^T v_c)^2.(i,c):Ωic​=1∑​(Zic​−uiT​vc​)2.

The small ridge makes each update solvable even when a row or column has fewer observations than components. Zero-filled singular vectors initialise the algorithm, but unobserved entries never enter its update objective. Automatic component selection currently applies reproducibility selection to the zero-filled stacked matrix; where coverage differs sharply, inspect that selection and compare prespecified counts.

After convergence, a rotation orders the fitted rank-one terms by the variance they carry. This does not make the factors unique: UVTUV^TUVT is the fitted reconstruction, and signs, rotations, or nearly equivalent low-rank solutions can describe similar data. The component share is the squared norm of its rank-one approximation over observed entries divided by the observed squared score norm. With missing entries these shares need not form an exact partition, because cross-terms over the observation mask may remain.

Stacking increases the number and diversity of observed rows and allows each concept to contribute wherever it is measured. It does not identify a direction merely because the vocabulary dimension exceeds one modality’s sample count.

Evidence for a joint component

For each component score, CHORALE fits a stacked linear model containing an intercept, modality, the phenotype terms, and covariates shared across modalities. The modality term absorbs cohort-level composition differences. Per-modality models are reported separately as descriptions.

Calibration again uses Freedman—Lane residual permutation, but exchange is always restricted within modality in addition to declared blocks. The component-specific p-value, max-component family-wise p-value, and permutation-based q-value have the same definitions as in the concept branch, with components replacing concepts.

Component orientation is arbitrary. Multiplying its scores and loadings by -1 reverses the effect sign but leaves the reconstruction and two-sided inference unchanged. Declare an orientation from loadings or an external convention before describing a component effect as positive or negative.

Leave-one-modality-out transfer

For held-out modality hhh, the state is fitted on all m≠hm \ne hm=h. Let Zh(C)Z_h^{(C)}Zh(C)​ contain the held-out scores for concepts also present in training loadings V(C)V^{(C)}V(C). Projection uses

Uh=Zh(C)V(C)(V(C)TV(C)+10−8I)−1.U_h = Z_h^{(C)} V^{(C)} \left(V^{(C)T}V^{(C)} + 10^{-8}I\right)^{-1}.Uh​=Zh(C)​V(C)(V(C)TV(C)+10−8I)−1.

The projected scores are tested in the held-out design with Freedman—Lane permutations. At least three modalities are required so the training set remains joint. The returned p_value is component-specific, not max-statistic or FDR control over the transfer table.

Each held-out fit has independent sign and order. Its loadings are matched one-to-one to a full-data reference by the assignment maximising absolute loading correlation. component is the reference label, fitted_component the local label, and loading_agreement the signed correlation. The reference supplies labels only. Effect signs from different held-out fits are comparable only after accounting for orientation.

Attribution among overlapping concepts

Concepts sharing features are not independent claims. For each concept, those whose Jaccard overlap with it in the measured features of a modality reaches a declared threshold are regressed out of its score, and the effect is re-estimated. The result is reported beside the original. A concept whose statistic survives carries the signal itself; one whose statistic collapses describes the neighbourhood it sits in.

Recovery against a known correspondence

Where two modalities were measured on the same individuals, the pairing can be withheld and used afterwards to check what the vocabulary asserts. The two modalities are presented under their own sample identifiers and encoded as though disjoint. The pairing then enters once, to compute the concept-by-concept agreement matrix AAA with acd=cor(SA,c,SB,d)a_{cd} = \mathrm{cor}(S_{A,c}, S_{B,d})acd​=cor(SA,c​,SB,d​) across the individuals both were measured on.

The correlation is signed. A concept score has a fixed orientation, since a larger score means a larger weighted mean of the features carrying the concept, so two modalities that rank the same biological state in opposite directions have not recovered it. Sign indeterminacy belongs to the free dimensions, which are not what is compared here.

Three quantities frame the result. The paired upper bound is the mean agreement of the optimal assignment of AAA, which is what any method knowing the pairing could reach. The random baseline is the mean agreement over one-to-one relabellings drawn at random. The recovery is the mean of the diagonal of AAA, the identity the vocabulary asserts, concept for concept by name, computed without a paired sample having been seen.

Random alignment is a baseline and not a bound: a vocabulary whose names do not track the same biology in the two modalities places below it, and the reported placement is negative rather than clipped.

Conditions of applicability

The vocabulary bounds what the concept channel expresses. Biology absent from it appears only in the free dimensions, and is named there by loadings rather than identified.

A concept expressible in two modalities and not in a third yields evidence across two. Coverage is reported per concept for that reason.

Curated concepts overlap, so attribution requires the overlap structure to be carried through the inference, which is what the family-wise permutation and the neighbour-adjusted statistic do.

ICA free dimensions rely on stable non-Gaussian structure. chorale_gates() reports non-Gaussianity and modality-difference diagnostics, but a diagnostic passing is not proof that an individual free axis is identified.

A coordinated phenotype shift supports an association with the named concept. Ordering cause and consequence requires timing, mediation or perturbation.

Literature

Independent component analysis and its identification conditions follow Comon (1994) and Hyvärinen and Oja (2000); the FastICA implementation is Marchini, Heaton and Ripley’s fastICA. Parallel analysis is Horn (1965). Linear assignment for matching components across runs uses the Hungarian algorithm through clue (Hornik 2005). Inverse-variance combination and Cochran’s Q are standard fixed-effect meta-analysis, as in Borenstein et al. (2009). The permutation null for a coefficient in a model carrying nuisance covariates is Freedman and Lane (1983), whose behaviour under the general linear model is reviewed by Winkler et al. (2014). Permutation-based false discovery estimation follows Tusher, Tibshirani and Chu (2001) and Storey and Tibshirani (2003); max-statistic family-wise control follows Westfall and Young (1993). Single-sample set scoring by the mean standardised level of a set’s members is the construction underlying Lee et al. (2008) and Hänzelmann, Castelo and Guinney (2013). Lipid shorthand names are parsed with Goslin (Kopczynski et al. 2020).

The observed-entry joint fit is an alternating regularised low-rank approximation. A reconstruction can be stable while a particular factor basis is not unique. The transfer analysis is a held-out assessment of the implemented fitted directions, not a separate identifiability theorem.
