How the estimator works

One common encoding followed by concept, family, joint-state, transfer, and free-dimension analyses.

CHORALE first creates one common representation and then asks several different questions of it. Keeping those questions separate is essential: they have different estimands, null distributions, and interpretations.

1. Fix the vocabulary and measure coverage

chorale_concepts() builds the vocabulary before any phenotype result is examined. Genes and proteins reach concepts through identifier mappings. Lipids reach compatible pathway concepts through lipid class. A custom named list of feature identifiers works as well.

Coverage is recorded for every concept and modality. “Not expressed” means that the modality contains fewer than the requested number of concept members. It does not mean that the biological process is absent.

2. Put each modality on an appropriate scale

chorale_transform() handles within-feature measurement scale: count-like data receive a variance-stabilising transform, skewed positive intensities receive log1p, and already symmetric data are left unchanged. Samples must already be normalised within modality for library size, total intensity, or the equivalent platform-specific exposure.

Each feature is then centred and scaled across the samples of its own modality. This prevents a high-abundance feature from dominating solely because of its unit or baseline.

3. Score the concepts without reading the phenotype

For each sample, a concept score is the weighted mean of the standardised features that carry that concept. Scores are standardised again within modality. chorale_encode() does not consult the design, so a phenotype contrast cannot choose or rotate the representation that will later be tested.

From here the workflow branches.

Branch A: named concept evidence

4A. Estimate an adjusted effect in each modality

chorale_concept_evidence() regresses each concept score on the phenotype and the covariates that all modalities can represent consistently. Continuous covariates contribute standardised slopes. Categorical covariates contribute treatment contrasts over shared levels.

The effect is measured in standard deviations of the concept score. An effect of 0.8 means that, after adjustment, the compared phenotype level is estimated to sit 0.8 concept-score standard deviations above the reference level.

5A. Combine, but keep disagreement visible

Effects are combined by inverse-variance weighting. A precise modality receives more weight; opposite directions cancel. sign_agreement reports the fraction of modalities pointing with the combined effect. heterogeneity_p tests for more disagreement than the standard errors predict. A small value warns against one common effect. A large value only says that disagreement was not detected; with few modalities the test has limited power.

6A. Calibrate and attribute

Freedman—Lane residual permutations hold the design fixed. The reduced model without the tested term is fitted, its residuals are exchanged within any declared exchangeability blocks, and the full model is refitted to the rebuilt score. One permutation is shared across concepts in a modality, preserving the dependence caused by overlapping feature sets.

p_value is concept-specific. p_family compares the concept with the largest statistic anywhere in the vocabulary and controls family-wise error. q_value estimates the false-discovery rate among concepts at least this extreme.

Overlapping neighbours are then regressed from each concept score. attributed_z asks whether the signal remains with that concept rather than with the neighbourhood it shares.

Branch B: families of related concepts

chorale_concept_families() groups concepts from their Jaccard overlap using complete linkage. Family construction uses only the vocabulary and therefore precedes testing. Every pair in a multi-concept family must meet the overlap threshold; ungrouped concepts remain as singleton families.

chorale_family_evidence() sums the signed concept statistics and divides by the square root of family size. Agreement accumulates and opposing directions cancel. Calibration reuses the signed per-concept permutation array, which is necessary because the family members overlap. A significant family means that its members move together as a group; it does not imply that every member is individually significant.

Branch C: one low-rank state over the stacked collection

chorale_joint_state() starts from the same concept scores but estimates a different object. Each concept is centred and scaled within each modality. Optional modality-local nuisance columns—such as a batch recorded only in one cohort—are regressed out before stacking. The phenotype is never allowed in that nuisance list.

The score blocks are stacked by row. A concept unavailable in one modality is stored as missing, not as zero, and carries no weight in the alternating ridge-regularised least-squares fit. The result is a low-rank approximation with one score per sample and one loading per concept.

Stacking supplies more observations and broader concept coverage than any single modality. It does not by itself identify a unique biological mechanism. Component sign is arbitrary, component order can change between fits, and different low-rank solutions may describe similar reconstructions. Interpret a component through its stability, loadings, phenotype evidence, per-modality effects, and transfer—not through its label alone.

chorale_joint_evidence() regresses the joint component scores on phenotype, shared covariates, and modality. The modality term prevents differences in cohort composition from masquerading as phenotype effects. Residual permutations take place within modality. The same component is also described within each modality, but those local effects are context, not a filtering rule.

Branch D: test the direction where it was not fitted

chorale_joint_transfer() requires at least three modalities. It leaves one modality out, fits the joint state on the remainder, projects the held-out concept scores onto those loadings, and tests the projected scores in the held-out design.

Each held-out decomposition has arbitrary order and sign. Components are matched one-to-one to a full-data reference by the absolute correlation of their concept loadings. loading_agreement retains the signed correlation. A negative value means that the held-out fit points opposite to the reference; the effect sign must be interpreted with that orientation. p_value is a component-specific permutation p-value, not a family-wise correction over all transferred components.

Transfer reduces one important explanation: structure unique to the held-out assay could not have produced loadings fitted without that assay. It still does not prove causality or eliminate confounding shared across studies.

Branch E: variation outside the vocabulary

The concept-score span is projected from each analysis matrix. ICA is fitted to the residual, producing free dimensions that are orthogonal to the concept scores. The count is selected by reproducibility under restarts and subsampling, bounded by parallel analysis and sample size.

If the realised concept-score matrix has full row rank, it spans the centred sample space and leaves no residual to decompose. Having at least as many concepts as samples makes full row rank possible, but does not guarantee it. An empty free-dimension table can therefore mean “not estimable because no residual remained,” not “tested and absent.” Read variance.tsv first.

What the controls do

Phenotype residual permutations calibrate the reported tests. The modality shuffle is descriptive because it changes feature support, cohort sizes, and design composition; it carries no p-value. Initialisation stability reports whether free dimensions reappear. chorale_concept_specificity() separately tests whether shared covariates drive the vocabulary as strongly as phenotype.

Controls do not rescue an unsuitable design. Exchangeability blocks, phenotype reference, nuisance variables, and the vocabulary are study decisions that must be justified before reading significance.
