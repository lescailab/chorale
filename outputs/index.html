Outputs and how to read them

Every table the report writes, what each column means, and what it does not establish.

chorale_report() writes report.html and machine-readable tables beside it. The concept files are always written. Family and joint files are written only when the corresponding already fitted objects are supplied. The HTML analysis status distinguishes “not supplied” from an empty or nonsignificant result.

The modalities were measured on different individuals, so what connects them is a fixed vocabulary of named biological concepts. A concept is a set of features that belong together for a stated biological reason, and every modality is scored on the same concepts. Every table below is organised by concept for that reason.

concepts.tsv: the primary result

One row per concept and phenotype contrast.

ColumnMeaning
concept, termThe concept and the phenotype contrast the row is about.
n_modalities, modalitiesHow many modalities express the concept, and which.
in_all_modalitiesWhether every modality of the collection expresses it.
effect_<m>, z_<m>The adjusted phenotype contrast in modality m, in standard deviations of the concept score, and its ratio to its standard error.
joint_effect, joint_zThe inverse-variance combination across the modalities that express the concept.
sign_agreementShare of those modalities whose effect points the same way as the combination.
heterogeneity_pCochran’s Q for the modalities being one quantity. A small value means they are not.
max_jaccard, attributed_zThe largest overlap with another concept, and the statistic that survives once overlapping concepts are regressed out.
p_valuePermutation p-value for this concept alone.
p_familyPermutation p-value against the largest statistic anywhere in the vocabulary.
q_valueFalse discovery rate over the vocabulary at this threshold.
significantWhether q_value clears the configured level.
beats_best_singleWhether the combination exceeds the best any one modality reached.

Four columns decide how much a supported concept is worth, and they answer different questions. joint_z says how strong the combined evidence is. sign_agreement says whether the modalities agree; a large statistic with agreement of 0.5 is one modality carrying the others. heterogeneity_p tests for excess disagreement. A small value warns against one common effect; a large value does not prove equality, especially with few modalities. attributed_z says whether the signal belongs to the concept or to the concepts it overlaps.

p_family and q_value control different things and neither is a correction applied to the other. p_family is the probability of any false call anywhere in the vocabulary; q_value is the share of calls expected to be false at that threshold. Both come from the same permutations, so both carry the dependence that overlapping concepts have rather than assuming it away.

concept_effects.tsv: one row per concept per modality

The unaggregated effects: effect, se and z per modality, with n_features, the number of features that modality has for the concept, and the same overlap columns computed within that modality. Read n_features before reading an effect. A concept a modality reaches through five features is a different measurement from one it reaches through five hundred.

concept_coverage.tsv: what could be tested at all

One row per concept per modality, carrying n_features and whether the modality expresses the concept at the configured threshold. This is the table that separates two situations a bare absence of results confuses: a collection carrying no shared signal, and a collection whose vocabulary reaches too little of the measured features to ask the question.

variance.tsv and concept_variance.tsv: what each channel holds

variance.tsv gives, per modality, the share of variance the concept scores reconstruct, the share the free dimensions carry, and the share left over. The three partition the variance of the analysis matrix. Most of the total is feature-level noise in any bulk assay, so the interesting comparison is between the first two rather than against one.

max_free_concept_correlation is a check on the construction rather than a result. The projection is onto the space the concept scores span, so the residual is orthogonal to every concept score and the value is zero to numerical precision. A value that is not near zero would mean the two channels are reporting the same signal twice.

concept_variance.tsv gives the share each concept carries on its own, in each modality.

A concept share of one, with n_free and free_ceiling both zero, says the realised concept-score matrix spans the centred sample space for that modality. Having more concepts than samples makes this possible but does not guarantee it. When the realised span is full, the concept scores reproduce the assay exactly and nothing is left to decompose, so the modality has no free channel in that run. Scoring the same collection on a vocabulary smaller than the modality’s sample count is what makes the question answerable.

free_dimensions.tsv: what the vocabulary cannot name

A fixed vocabulary bounds what the concept channel can express. Coordinated variation outside it is carried here.

ColumnMeaning
modality, dimensionWhich free dimension the row is about.
variance_shareShare of the modality’s variance this dimension reconstructs. The dimensions of a modality sum to the free share the channel carries; where they cannot, no per-dimension share is reported.
reproducibilityMean matched correlation with the same dimension recovered from other initialisations.
effect, se, zThe adjusted phenotype contrast on the dimension.
p_value, p_familyCalibrated from rebuilt score residuals with the design fixed; p_family is taken against the largest statistic over every free dimension.
outside_vocabularyWhether the dimension is called at the configured level.
top_featuresThe features loading on it most heavily.

top_features describes a dimension; it does not identify it. A free dimension has no name, and naming it from its loadings is the reader’s inference rather than the method’s output.

The concept channel is fitted first, so the free dimensions hold only what is orthogonal to every concept score. A phenotype-linked direction that shares its direction with a concept is carried by that concept and does not appear here.

added_value.tsv: whether integration was needed

joint_z beside best_single_z, their difference as margin, and needs_multiple. A concept only one modality expresses cannot need more than one modality, whatever its statistic, and is marked accordingly. A positive margin is what separates a result integration produced from a result one modality produced and the others accompanied.

controls.tsv: what would have caught an artefact

One row per control, with the smallest p-value that control can attain beside its result.

ControlWhat it breaksWhen it is inapplicable
Phenotype permutationThe part of the score the adjusting covariates do not explain, with the design held fixedNever; it is the calibration the reported p-values come from.
Modality shuffleThe assignment of samples to modalities, after poolingAlways, as a test. It is reported as a description.
Initialisation stabilityNothing; it reports how reproducibly the free dimensions were recoveredWhere no free dimensions were fitted.

The modality shuffle carries a median statistic and no p-value. Three differences separate a shuffled collection from the fit it would be read against. Pooling keeps only the features the modalities share, so the shuffled collections are scored on a smaller feature space and often on a smaller vocabulary. The reallocation preserves neither each modality’s sample count nor its phenotype and covariate composition. The observed statistic was computed on the full assays. The median across shuffles describes how much of the result survives that treatment, and nothing about it is an error rate. An inferential form needs the observed statistic refitted on the common features alone, feature spaces that can be pooled, and modality labels permuted within declared design cells with each modality keeping its count in every cell.

A control that could not be computed is named with its reason rather than passed over. Reading smallest_attainable_p before quoting a p-value is what stops a control being read against a threshold it could never reach.

chorale_concept_specificity() runs a fourth check, separately, because it produces a table rather than a row. Each covariate the modalities share is tested in the phenotype’s place, over the same vocabulary and against the same null, so the phenotype result can be read against what the procedure recovers when something else drives it. A covariate is tested on the terms it contributes to the model: a contrast per level for a categorical covariate, a standardised slope for a continuous one. Anchors are compared on the family-wise p-value rather than on the largest statistic. A covariate with four levels contributes three contrasts, and so three times as many chances at a large maximum. A vocabulary whose evidence under the phenotype is no stronger than under sex or age is reporting cohort structure.

fit.rds: the whole result

The fitted object, written with saveRDS() and reloaded with readRDS(). It carries the concept scores, the free dimensions, the vocabulary and its coverage, the evidence table and the control object that produced them, so an analysis resumes from it without the tables having to be lossless.

concept_scores_<m>.tsv and free_scores_<m>.tsv

One row per sample, one column per concept or free dimension. These are the quantities every table above is computed from, so they are what a downstream analysis of the same collection starts from.

concept_families.tsv and family_evidence.tsv

These files appear when family_evidence is supplied to chorale_report().

concept_families.tsv is the audit trail for construction: one row per concept, with family and family_size. A singleton is retained rather than discarded. family_evidence.tsv contains one row per family and phenotype term.

ColumnMeaning
statisticAbsolute signed sum of member z-statistics divided by the square root of family size.
mean_zMean signed member statistic; its sign is meaningful because concept scores have fixed orientation.
best_single_zLargest absolute statistic of one member, for comparison with the group result.
p_valueFamily-specific permutation p-value.
p_familyP-value against the largest family statistic in each permutation.
q_valueEstimated FDR over the tested families.
membersSemicolon-separated concepts contributing to the row.

A supported family says its members move coherently as a group. It does not say that every listed concept is individually supported or independently measured.

Joint-state audit files

These files appear when joint_state is supplied.

  • joint_scores.tsv: sample_id, modality, and one score per component.
  • joint_loadings.tsv: concept and its signed loading on each component.
  • joint_variance.tsv: the observed-entry rank-one share per component.
  • joint_coverage.tsv: samples and observed concepts contributed by modality.
  • joint_nuisance.tsv: modality-local covariates removed before stacking.

Component signs are arbitrary. A negative loading is meaningful only relative to other loadings in the same chosen orientation. The component shares describe rank-one fitted energy over observed entries; with missing concept blocks they need not sum to an exact partition.

joint_components.tsv and joint_component_effects.tsv

These appear when joint_evidence is supplied. joint_components.tsv carries the combined adjusted effect, standard error, z-statistic, component share, component-specific p-value, max-component p_family, permutation q_value, and the two significance flags. joint_component_effects.tsv gives the same adjusted effect within each modality as context.

The joint effect sign reverses if the component is reoriented. Read magnitude and calibrated error rates first, then define direction from loadings. A component can be significant jointly while being weak in one modality; local effects are reported, not used as a hidden inclusion rule.

joint_transfer.tsv

This appears when joint_transfer is supplied. Each row gives the held-out modality, training modalities, reference component, local fitted_component, signed loading_agreement, concept and sample counts, and the held-out effect, uncertainty, z-statistic, and p-value.

The p-value is component-specific and not corrected over the whole transfer table. Rows sharing component have been matched to the same full-data reference direction. If loading_agreement is negative, the local orientation is reversed; compare effect signs only after accounting for that reversal. Low absolute agreement means the reference label is weak even if the held-out component has a small p-value.

Reading absence correctly

StateMeaning
File not written and HTML says not suppliedThe caller did not include that fitted analysis. No statistical conclusion follows.
File written with zero rowsThe analysis was supplied but produced no estimable row. Read coverage, component count, and prerequisites.
Rows present and not significantThe quantity was tested and did not beat the stated null at the available permutation resolution.
Smallest attainable p-value above the decision thresholdThe resampling plan could not express the desired claim, regardless of the observed statistic.

What a supported concept establishes

A coordinated phenotype shift in a named piece of biology, seen in the modalities that express the concept, at a stated error rate. Ordering cause and consequence requires timing, mediation or perturbation. Attribution to the concept rather than to its neighbourhood is what attributed_z reports, and a concept whose statistic collapses there describes the region of the vocabulary it sits in.
